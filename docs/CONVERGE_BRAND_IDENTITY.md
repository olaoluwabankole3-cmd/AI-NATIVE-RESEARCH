# Converge — Brand Identity

## Core idea

The Converge icon is a **bold capital C with lines converging behind its left edge**. The C stands for Converge; the rays represent people, disciplines and ideas moving toward a shared point. This icon is the primary symbol for app icons, compact spaces and favicons.

The wordmark is lowercase **converge.**, with bold, unfussy geometric lettering. The mint dot is an optional accent in the main green and white-on-dark lockups.

## Rules

1. Use **white + mint on Night Forest** for dark interfaces and the main app icon.
2. Use **deep green + mint on white or pale backgrounds**.
3. Keep the C in front of the rays, allowing the ray tips to disappear slightly behind the C's left stroke. Do not replace the C with a central dot or network node.
4. Do not stretch, crop or redraw the rays independently.
5. The lowercase wordmark is `converge.`; use the icon alone when space is tight.
6. Keep the approved public landing-page animation intact when updating brand assets.
