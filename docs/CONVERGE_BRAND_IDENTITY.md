# Converge — Brand Identity

## Main logo and header logo

The approved main logo is the lowercase **converge.** wordmark with three converging rays on the left and three on the right. The rays frame the lettering. Preserve this complete lockup; do not replace the left-hand rays with the C icon or insert a separate C into the wordmark.

## Standalone icon

The standalone icon is a bold capital **C** with lines converging behind its left edge. Use it only as a separate app icon, favicon or symbol-only mark. This was the only part of the logo requested to change.

## Logo files

- `public/converge-header-lockup.svg`: white wordmark with mint rays, used in the dark signed-in and public marketing headers.
- `public/converge-header-lockup-green.svg`: green wordmark and rays with a mint dot for light backgrounds.
- `public/converge-wordmark.svg`: primary green version of the same side-ray logo.
- `public/converge-wordmark-dark.svg`: white wordmark and mint rays for dark backgrounds.
- `public/converge-wordmark-white.svg`: all-white monochrome wordmark.
- `public/converge-wordmark-black.svg`: charcoal monochrome wordmark.
- `public/converge-wordmark-mint.svg`: mint monochrome wordmark.
- `public/converge-icon-green.svg`, `public/converge-icon-white.svg`, `public/converge-icon-dark.svg` and `public/converge-icon-mint.svg`: separate C-and-rays icons.

## Rules

1. The side-ray **converge.** wordmark remains the main and header logo.
2. Use white text and mint rays on dark surfaces; use deep green on light surfaces.
3. The C-and-rays symbol remains separate from the wordmark.
4. Preserve the approved landing-page particle animation when updating static logo assets.
