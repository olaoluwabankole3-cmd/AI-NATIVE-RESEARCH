# Converge — Brand Identity

## Core symbol
The icon is a **bold capital C with three lines converging behind its left edge**. The C sits in front of the rays; their tips should visually disappear behind the letter. Do not replace the symbol with a network node, cross, or multi-dot mark.

## Wordmark
Use the lowercase **converge.** wordmark, with bold geometric lettering. The horizontal lockup places the C/rays symbol to the left of the wordmark.

## Preferred colourways
- **Dark interface:** white C and wordmark with mint rays on Night Forest (#0B1110).
- **Light interface:** deep-green logo (#137C79) on white, with mint dot accent (#48B99A).
- **Monochrome:** all-white on dark backgrounds, all-charcoal on light backgrounds.
- **Pale mint:** deep-green symbol on Pale Mint (#E6F5EC).

## Usage
1. Use white + mint on Night Forest for dark interfaces and the primary app icon.
2. Use deep green on white or pale-mint backgrounds.
3. Keep the C in front of the rays so the rays disappear behind the letter.
4. Do not stretch or alter the ray angles individually.
5. Leave the approved landing-page animation intact when updating logo assets.
